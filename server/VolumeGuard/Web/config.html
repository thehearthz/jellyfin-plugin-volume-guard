Volume Guard

Set shared listening profiles and a weekly night schedule for your companion TV player.

Requires the Volume Guard Android TV companion. This plugin cannot add controls to the stock Jellyfin TV app. Audio processing runs in the companion player, where audio passthrough is disabled so audio can be processed. These settings do not indicate that processing is active.

Playback defaults

Turning this off makes the companion use unprocessed audio, including during scheduled hours.

Auto uses Night during scheduled hours and your daytime mode at other times. A player override takes precedence.

Balanced levels quieter passages gently. Night uses a quieter target and a lower peak ceiling.

Night schedule

For example, America/New_York, Europe/London, or UTC. Daylight saving follows this time zone.

Days are the days each window starts. Friday 22:00 to 07:00 ends Saturday morning. End times are exclusive; equal start and end times are not allowed.

No night windows. Auto will use the daytime mode.

Advanced audio profiles

These are signal levels, not a room volume limit or measured LUFS loudness. Higher maximum gain can raise background noise. Keep the target at or below the peak ceiling.
